# Datasets and Benchmarks

This section contains datasets and benchmarks that can be used to evaluate
the factual accuracy, scientific knowledge, and question-answering ability
of Large Language Models.

## 1. SciFact

**Source:** Allen Institute for AI (AI2)

**Description:** SciFact is a scientific claim verification dataset. It
contains scientific claims together with evidence from scientific
documents. Systems must determine whether the evidence supports or
contradicts a claim.

**Application:** Scientific fact verification and evaluation of whether
language models can correctly verify scientific claims against evidence.

[Dataset and Code](https://github.com/allenai/scifact)

---

## 2. PubMedQA

**Source:** PubMedQA Research Project

**Description:** PubMedQA is a biomedical research question-answering
dataset. Questions are answered using information from PubMed research
article abstracts. It contains expert-labeled, unlabeled, and artificially
generated question-answer pairs.

**Application:** Evaluating the ability of language models to answer
biomedical research questions accurately using scientific evidence.

[Dataset Website](https://pubmedqa.github.io/)

[Dataset and Code](https://github.com/pubmedqa/pubmedqa)

---

## 3. GPQA

**Source:** Graduate-Level Google-Proof Q&A Benchmark

**Description:** GPQA is a challenging benchmark containing graduate-level
questions in scientific domains including Biology, Physics, and Chemistry.
The questions are designed to test deep scientific knowledge rather than
simple information retrieval.

**Application:** Benchmarking the scientific knowledge and reasoning
accuracy of Large Language Models across difficult scientific questions.

[Dataset and Code](https://github.com/idavidrein/gpqa)
